With exams creeping closer, MECHA took a much-needed wellness break at Auckland Climbing Gym, Parnell. The goal was simple: step away from the study grind, move a little, and enjoy a relaxed afternoon together.

Students bounced between different bouldering routes, trying out problems that ranged from easy warmups to trickier holds that needed a bit of engineering creativity. The atmosphere stayed super chill with lots of laughs, casual competition, and everyone climbing at their own pace. It was a great reminder that taking a moment to unwind can make the busy weeks ahead feel a bit more manageable.

A big thanks to Auckland Climbing Gym for hosting us and providing such a welcoming space!
